# Forge: ChatGPT-first master plan

**Status:** canonical product and implementation plan

## 1. Product decision

Forge exists to let a normal ChatGPT coding conversation operate on a real repository after Codex limits are exhausted.

The winning experience is not the most sophisticated Cloudflare architecture. It is the experience with the fewest concepts, the lowest monthly cost, the smallest context load and the shortest route from conversation to tested pull request.

The product promise is:

> ChatGPT does the reasoning. Forge gives ChatGPT a safe, cheap development computer
