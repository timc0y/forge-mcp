# Forge 0.5 plan

The canonical all-in-one product and delivery plan is [PRODUCT-PLAN.md](./PRODUCT-PLAN.md).

Forge 0.5 is presented and accepted as one complete repository-to-review-to-PR product. The plan does not publish phases. Individual capabilities may be disabled in the private pilot until their security gate passes, but they remain part of one product contract and one end-to-end acceptance test.
